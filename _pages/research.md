## Job Market Paper

<div class="research-card">

### **Get Fit with MA? Estimating the Impact of Medicare Advantage on Physical Activity**
**with Jing Li**

[Paper](https://jason-jongmin-jung.com/files/Jung_Li_2026.pdf)

This paper studies whether Medicare Advantage enrollment increases physical activity around the age-65 Medicare eligibility threshold. Using nationally representative survey data and a regression discontinuity difference-in-differences design, we find a larger increase in daily exercise among MA enrollees relative to the comparison group, with stronger responses among women, non-White adults, and adults without a college degree.

**Topics:** Medicare Advantage · Physical Activity · Healthy Aging · Causal Inference

### **Move to Move? Neighborhood Fitness Culture and Exercise Among Older Adults**

This paper studies whether moving to neighborhoods with different fitness cultures changes exercise behavior among older adults. Using HRS movers linked to restricted ZIP-code information, the analysis combines mover-based comparisons with event-study evidence.

### **Do Unionized Firms Have Better Management Practices?**
**with Arnab Basu**

[Paper](https://jason-jongmin-jung.com/files/Jung_Basu_2026.pdf)

Using restricted World Management Survey data, we examine how unionization relates to management practices across manufacturing firms in North America, Latin America, and Europe. Unionized firms perform better on structured operations, monitoring, and target-setting practices but worse on people-management practices.
